# CIE 1931 observer data

`CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv` is an unmodified copy of the International Commission
on Illumination (CIE), 2019, *Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard
colorimetric observer*, DOI [10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b](https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b).
The original metadata accompanies the CSV.

Source: [CIE dataset page](https://cie.co.at/datatable/cie-1931-colour-matching-functions-2-degree-observer).
Downloaded 2026-09-27 from
[the CIE CSV](https://files.cie.co.at/Publications-datasets/CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv)
and [metadata](https://files.cie.co.at/Publications-datasets/CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv_metadata.json).
SHA-256: `fa663e3535a7e0763a745993a1f0a192eb0275ac46ad2d1befd7626841e713c1`.

The table contains the 2-degree observer's x, y and z matching functions at
1 nm intervals from 360 to 830 nm. `colorimetry.py` integrates Planck radiation
against this table, normalises Y, converts XYZ to linear sRGB, clips negative
RGB channels, and renormalises luminance for a nonnegative light tint.
